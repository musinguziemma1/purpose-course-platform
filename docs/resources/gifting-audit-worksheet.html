Gifting Audit Worksheet

Use specific examples to explore your strengths. This is a reflection exercise: its purpose is to generate useful questions and small experiments, not a fixed label.

Name:Date:

1. What do people repeatedly ask me to help with?

Describe two recent situations. What did you do, and what changed?

2. Where have I produced a useful result?

Name the task, the result, and evidence someone else could observe.

3. Which worthwhile activities hold my attention?

Distinguish an activity you enjoy from an ability you have practised. Where do they overlap?

4. What feedback has appeared more than once?

Record the words used and the examples behind them, including a useful challenge.

Use examples from work, home, study, community service or creative practice. You can leave any question unanswered.

Consider the people, setting, preparation and support that help your strengths show up.

Choose one limitation that affects your contribution and a realistic way to practise.
