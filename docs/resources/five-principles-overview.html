The Five Purpose Principles

A practical map for reflection and action. Work with real experiences, ask honest questions, and return to these principles as you learn.

01 · Identity — Who am I?

Notice your values, strengths and responsibilities. Describe yourself beyond a job title, achievement or other person's expectations.

Reflect: When have my choices expressed what matters most to me?

02 · Source — Where am I from?

Consider the beliefs, relationships, heritage and experiences that have shaped you. Name what you want to honour, question or change.

Reflect: What have I inherited, and how will I respond to it?

03 · Purpose — Why am I here?

Look for the people and needs you are willing to serve. Form a working statement of contribution, then test it through action.

Reflect: Whose life could improve through the contribution I can make?

04 · Potential — What can I do?

Gather evidence of your gifts and skills. Choose one ability to practise, one limitation to address, and one person who can offer useful feedback.

Reflect: What small task would let me test this ability in a real setting?

05 · Destiny — Where am I going?

Translate a meaningful direction into milestones, habits and review dates. Let evidence and changing responsibilities inform the next step.

Reflect: What result do I want to work towards, and what will I do this week?
